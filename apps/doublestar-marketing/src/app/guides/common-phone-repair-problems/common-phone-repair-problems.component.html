5 Common Phone Repair Problems (and How We Fix Them)

Here are the issues we see most often at Double Star, what usually causes them, and what a typical fix involves.

1. Cracked or shattered screen

Usually caused by a drop. If the display still shows an image and touch still responds, it's almost always a straightforward screen replacement.

2. Battery drains quickly or the phone switches off unexpectedly

A battery naturally loses capacity over time. If your phone struggles to last half a day, or shuts down suddenly at 20–30%, a battery replacement usually resolves it.

3. Phone won't charge, or charges very slowly

Often lint or debris in the charging port rather than a hardware fault — we clean it first before recommending a port replacement.

4. Water or liquid damage

Turn the phone off immediately and don't charge it. The sooner it's brought in, the better the chance of a full recovery.

5. Cracked back glass or housing

Mostly cosmetic, but exposed edges can let in dust and moisture — worth fixing sooner rather than later.
